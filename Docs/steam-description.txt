[b]All the Progression goodness, less research.[/b]

[i]This is a fork of [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3690565613]Progression: Factories[/url] by ferny. Huge thanks to ferny for the original mod and all the Progression work this builds on. For a really good modpack, check out [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3521297585]The Progression Modpack[/url].[/i]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Overhauls factory research, so each Vanilla Furniture Expanded - Factory machine has a place in the research tree.
[*]Automated machines follow Fabrication, and the larger factory machines follow VFE Factory's basic factories.
[/list]

[h2]Why "LESS"?[/h2]
Progression: Factories adds up to [b]21 research projects[/b] depending on your mods. This fork groups them into [b]at most 5[/b]:
[list]
[*][b]After Fabrication:[/b] Automated extraction, Automated food production, Automated crafting
[*][b]After basic factories:[/b] Factory processing, Factory manufacturing
[/list]
The factory booster and heatsink return to Underground conveyor belts. Every machine stays at the same stage, and still needs the other research it did before. The research sits in a compact block beside VFE Factory's own projects.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Keeps all of the original's mod support:
[list]
[*]Vanilla Furniture Expanded - Factory and No Factory Floor Requirement (both required)
[*]Medieval Overhaul
[*]VQE Drone Factory
[/list]

[b]Replaces the original. Don't use both.[/b]

[b]Switching mid-game isn't recommended.[/b] Many of the original's research projects are merged or removed, so swapping Progression: Factories for this fork in an existing save can lose research progress. Start a new game when you switch.
